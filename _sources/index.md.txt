# Pereval documentation

Welcome to the official documentation of Pereval!

Pereval is a toolkit for evaluating MIDI piano performances. It supports the recently proposed deep feature metrics described in the paper ["Integrating Contextual Embeddings into Evaluation of Expressive MIDI Piano Performances"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27909) (Dmitrii Gavrilev, Ilya Borovik, and Vladimir Viro, Accepted at ISMIR 2026).
